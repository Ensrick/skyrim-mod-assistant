## Plugin and load-order audit

`VLIOVarinia.esp` is a full ESP, not an ESL. It contains 2,568 records, including
2,533 new records, 617 dialogue topics, 1,621 dialogue responses, and 23 quests.
It cannot safely be ESL-flagged or compacted: it contains new `CELL`, `DIAL`,
and `INFO` records and its FormIDs are outside the light-plugin range.

There are 35 vanilla overrides: 18 cells, 12 placed NPCs, three placed objects,
the Tamriel worldspace record, and `MQ201` (Diplomatic Immunity). The cell and
worldspace records are principally containers for Varinia's persistent
references, but still participate in ordinary winner semantics for cell data.

After the current LOOT sort, Varinia loads after USSEP and Lux Orbis, but before
Lux Orbis CS, Skyrim Unbound, NFF, Lux, and Water for ENB. This is sound for the
present profile:

- all selected exterior-cell data shared with Lux Orbis is identical;
- Lux Orbis CS wins its later Community Shaders exterior changes;
- Lux wins every overlapping interior-lighting cell;
- Water for ENB wins its overlapping water/cell data;
- Lux restores the USSEP ownership fix for Erikur's House that Varinia would
  otherwise revert;
- the four placed-NPC records shared with USSEP are semantically identical
  (one rotation differs only by floating-point serialization noise); and
- no active plugin other than Varinia currently overrides `MQ201`.

Any future AI overhaul, city/interior overhaul, Diplomatic Immunity overhaul,
or NPC schedule package touching these records requires a fresh winner audit.
No custom compatibility patch is presently justified.

The active-profile asset scan found zero path conflicts between Varinia's 2,429
BSA entries and the loose or archived assets supplied by the 100 enabled MO2
mods. Some bundled third-party texture paths are not namespaced, so this check
must be repeated if KS Hairdos, Common Clothing Expansion, Royal Elven Armor,
Mihail Crows, or the credited source mods are later installed separately.

MO2 audit, master/load-order audit, ledger verification, and LOOT all pass.

## Script audit

The main BSA contains 317 compiled PEX files and 324 PSC source files. Most are
Creation Kit dialogue fragments. The custom scripts use only vanilla Papyrus
types and do not import SKSE, PapyrusUtil, JContainers, ConsoleUtil, NFF, or AFT.

The official archive has a concrete packaging defect: six dialogue-fragment
scripts are attached in `VLIOVarinia.esp` and present as source, but their
compiled PEX files are absent:

- `VLIO_TIF__050F8A8A` - dismiss Moe and clear the crow state;
- `VLIO_TIF__050F8A8C` - dismiss Hedy and clear the crow state;
- `VLIO_TIF__050F8A8E` - dismiss Mata and clear the crow state;
- `VLIO_TIF__050FDB94` - remove Varinia's concentration spell;
- `VLIO_TIF__050FDB98` - remove Varinia's aimed spell; and
- `VLIO_TIF__050FDB9C` - remove Fast Healing.

Those six dialogue actions are therefore expected not to execute. A seventh
uncompiled source fragment, `VLIO_TIF__051B4278`, is not referenced by the
plugin and is harmless source debris. `VLIODisguiseScript` contains a debug
notification, but the current plugin does not attach that compiled script, so
it is dormant.

Nexus shows zero formal bug reports for 1.1.0. The missing compiled fragments
were found from the shipped archive rather than from a user report. A full
source/VMAD/historical-package audit subsequently proved this is a packaging /
generated-source defect rather than intentionally disabled content. The private
six-PEX overlay was compiled, bytecode-validated, installed, and enabled on
2026-08-30. See `records/varinia-private-fragment-fix-2026-08-30.md`.

## Permissions and public-pack policy

The Nexus permissions prohibit re-uploading the file, require permission to
modify it, require permission to reuse its assets, prohibit paid reuse, and
prohibit Donation Points for derivative files. Several assets also belong to
credited third parties. The author additionally forbids training AI on the
voice files.

The archive and installed BSAs/ESP are restricted vendor inputs. A public list
or installer may reference Nexus mod 148853 and exact file 697835, but may not
bundle the archive or extracted files. Compiling and distributing the missing
PEX fragments is a derivative modification and requires author/asset
permission. The installed correction remains private and is not committed or
approved for public distribution.
