# Vanilla books with prose, alphabetical (2026-09-08)

Classified by the in-game mesh: `note0*.nif` and `rollleather*` are notes, `journallowpoly*` are journals, `spelltome*` are spell books. What remains - `basicbook*`, `book02lowpoly*` and friends - are the bound volumes this list covers. Records with under 20 words of text are excluded, as are duplicate titles (the same book placed by several records).

402 unique titles, 337,817 words total, median 611 words.

How this was built: `skyrim-record-cli record-selected-fields-by-type <plugin> Book Name,BookText,Model` over Skyrim.esm, Dawnguard.esm, Dragonborn.esm and HearthFires.esm, English strings only, HTML tags stripped before counting.

Full corpus by mesh: **1,049 BOOK records** - 442 bound books, 364 notes, 133 spell tomes, 110 journals. So two thirds of what the game calls a BOOK is not a book.

One row is a Bethesda font test (`123 Abc Def Ghijklmnop...`) and should be dropped from any target set. Skill books are included here and must keep teaching their perk on first read.
