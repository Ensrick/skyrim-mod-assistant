## Distribution boundary

RDO Lite prohibits reupload, modification, and asset reuse without permission.
Do not bundle its ESP, BSA, voice, or scripts. Skyrim Unbound and NFF impose
the same no-reupload boundary on the selected compatibility files. A public
installer may fetch the authorized Nexus archives and reproduce these exact
selections, but the collection repository and release artifacts must not carry
the extracted vendor files.
