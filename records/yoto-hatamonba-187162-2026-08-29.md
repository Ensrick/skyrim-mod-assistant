# Katana - Yoto Hatamonba 187162 deep audit

Audited headlessly on 2026-08-29/30 as a prospective signature weapon for a
future named NPC. Nothing was installed, no game or mod-manager UI was opened,
and no curator, Keep, Skip, plugin-list, or load-order state was changed.

## Decision

**HOLD / conditional Keep. Do not install the current Vexcation port as-is.**

The art is distinctive and technically economical enough to retain as a
candidate: it has complete 1K-or-lower textures, a modeled scabbard, working
weapon collision, first- and third-person resources, and only about 6,061
triangles across its ten shapes. It could make an excellent unique NPC weapon.

Neither published SSE plugin meets this list's engineering or balance standard,
however. The current Vexcation port still ships Oldrim-format NIFs, an
unnecessary Dragonsreach cell override, two duplicate gameplay variants,
trivial forge recipes, and an effectively unlimited absorb-health enchantment.
The newer RisingDragonRonin remaster has genuinely converted and compressed
assets, but its plugin is a full ESP with a self-referential base enchantment,
and its two-handed NIF has one wrong environment-map path.

If the user elects to Keep the weapon, the preferred integration is:

1. use the **non-bloody V1** file from RisingDragonRonin as an immutable external
   asset dependency;
2. leave its vendor ESP disabled;
3. provide one Ensrick-owned ESL containing a single balanced two-handed weapon,
   with no forge recipe, world placement, or leveled-list distribution;
4. place any corrected vendor NIF in a separate, attributed overlay rather than
   modifying the vendor archive; and
5. assign the weapon only through a later, separate named-NPC distribution
   patch.

This is a recommendation, not authorization to perform that integration.

## Vexcation plugin audit

`YotoHatamonba.esp` is a 3,074-byte, form-version-44 plugin with only
`Skyrim.esm` as a master. It is correctly Small/ESL-flagged: every new local
FormID is within `0x800` through `0xFFF`. It contains eleven records:

| Local FormID | Signature | Editor ID / purpose |
|---:|---|---|
| `000800` | COBJ | `RecipeWeaponqiwenchang1h` |
| `000801` | COBJ | `TemperWeaponqiwenchang1h` |
| `000802` | COBJ | `RecipeWeaponqiwenchang2h` |
| `000803` | COBJ | `TemperWeaponqiwenchang2h` |
| `000804` | MGEF | `EnchAbsorbHealthFFContactYoto` |
| `000805` | ENCH | `EnchYotoAbsorbHealth` |
| `000806` | STAT | `001Yoto2H` |
| `000D62` | WEAP | `QIWenChang1H` |
| `000D63` | STAT | `001QiWenChang1S` |
| `000D65` | WEAP | `QIWenChang2H` |
| `0165A3` | CELL override | `WhiterunDragonsreach` |

The CELL override contains no placed references and contributes no weapon
distribution. It carries localized/name data that the weapon does not need and
has historically been associated with altered Dragonsreach door text. It must
not survive into an owned integration. It is described here as an unnecessary
override rather than assigning a precise xEdit ITM count: the headless audit
used Mutagen record/link comparison and Spriggit serialization, not an official
xEdit Quick Auto Clean run.

Both Mutagen and a full Spriggit 0.41 binary round trip succeeded. Across 56
record links, there were zero unresolved links. No deleted records or deleted
references were present. There are no scripts.

| Weight | 5 | 12 |
| Value | 8,000 | 8,000 |
| Critical damage | 8 | 12 |
| Animation/type | one-handed sword | two-handed greatsword |

Both records carry sword/vendor/material keywords and
`IgnoresNormalWeaponResistance`. That flag and the stated value should be
removed for a balanced player-obtainable signature weapon.

The custom enchantment, **Blood Drink**, absorbs 20 health for one second on
hit. It is NoAutoCalc, has zero enchantment cost, and the WEAP records contain
no usable finite charge amount. In practice this is an unlimited absorb-health
effect, and its power is compounded by the unusually fast weapon speeds. The
effect description is also low-quality English rather than finished setting
lore.

The weapon is not in a leveled list, container, NPC inventory, or world
reference. Its only acquisition route is generic crafting:

- 1H: one leather strip and two steel ingots;
- 2H: two leather strips and three steel ingots; and
- tempering: one steel ingot, with Arcane Blacksmith required for the enchanted
  item.

Both forge recipes conflict with the intended unique-NPC use and should be
omitted. A tempering recipe may remain if the eventual NPC weapon is lootable.

## Mesh, texture, UV, and material audit

The Vexcation archive contains two NIFs and fourteen DDS files. Contrary to the
page's cleaned-mesh implication, both NIFs remain Oldrim Gamebryo stream 83
(`20.2.0.7`, user version 12). Each uses ten `NiTriShape` blocks and zero
`BSTriShape` blocks. They are structurally readable and their geometry is
triangulated, but they were not actually resaved to the SSE stream-100 format.

The source-built headless `nif-port-cli` converted both Vexcation meshes to
stream 100/`BSTriShape` without losing shapes, collision, or texture paths, and
the converted files reloaded successfully. A proper conversion is therefore
feasible, but the test output remains an ignored audit artifact and was not
installed.

Each supplied mesh contains:

- ten rendered shapes, approximately 4,657 vertices and 6,061 triangles total;
- complete finite normals, tangents, and UVs;
- a custom sheath/scabbard;
- `bhkCollisionObject`, rigid-body, list-shape, transform-shape, and box-shape
  collision data;
- six lighting shader properties, four effect shader properties, six texture
  sets, and five alpha properties; and
- animated glow/effect controllers plus blade/sheath environment mapping.

Every custom texture path resolves inside the archive. The package includes one
128x128 cubemap, 256x512 fittings, a 1024x128 trim, and 1024x1024 blade/scabbard
maps, all with complete mip chains. Nothing exceeds the list's 4K ceiling; the
principal weapon surfaces are an appropriate 1K. The Vexcation DDS files are
byte-identical to the 2014 source and retain old, mostly uncompressed formats,
which explains the roughly 20.6 MB extracted size. This is not a performance
problem for one weapon, but it is not a modern remaster either.

The third-person WEAP model and its first-person STAT model resolve to the
corresponding 1H or 2H asset, so both viewpoints are covered. They are not
separately optimized art variants; the same perspective-appropriate weapon NIF
is reused through Skyrim's normal model references.

The NIF `Prn` attachment strings are important for animation/display planning:

- 1H: `WeaponSword`, the vanilla hip sword location;
- 2H: `WeaponBack`, the vanilla two-handed back location.

The 2H mesh therefore does **not** provide a Japanese waist/obi scabbard
position. Any distinctive scabbard placement and matching draw/sheath animation
would be a separate IED/OAR animation project. The mesh contains no evidence
that such an animation package is included.

## RisingDragonRonin comparison

RisingDragonRonin's v2.0.0 files are the better technical asset base. Their NIFs
are genuine stream-100 SSE files with `BSTriShape` geometry and no unknown
blocks. The DDS files retain the source resolutions and mip chains but are BC7
compressed, reducing the archive to roughly 4.4 MB. V2 adds two blood-edge
shapes; V1 preserves the cleaner original blade and is preferred for this list.

The remaster does not make its plugin suitable as shipped:

- it is a ten-record full ESP, not ESL-flagged;
- its new records begin around `0x12C3`, so setting the ESL flag without
  compaction would be invalid;
- it still supplies both 1H and 2H variants and generic forge recipes;
- it retains the same excessive weapon values and general balance;
- its `EnchWeaponAbsorbHealthYoto` ENCH record points its Base Enchantment field
  back to itself, a malformed self-reference;
- its custom MGEF record is unused while the enchantment instead uses a vanilla
  absorb-health effect; and
- its 2H `GuiKe` shape points environment-map slot 5 to `tiao_m.dds`, although
  both the source mesh and Rising's own readme specify `jinke002_m.dds`.

Rising did remove the unwanted Dragonsreach CELL override. Its plugin otherwise
round-trips through Spriggit and resolves all 49 links, and it has no deleted
records. `Update.esm` appears unnecessary as a master based on the resolved-link
graph, though that should be confirmed when constructing a fresh owned record
rather than cleaning the vendor plugin.

## Safe two-handed conversion and proposed normalization

The current all-katanas-two-handed patcher recognizes the weapon because its
name, EditorID, and asset path contain strong katana tokens. Running it directly
against either vendor plugin is not safe: it would convert the already existing
1H record and also reprocess the supplied 2H record, leaving duplicate weapons.
Its generic damage scaling would turn damage 12 into 17, which is reasonable,
but would turn the existing damage-24 version into 34, which is not.

Use one source record only, or construct the owned 2H WEAP directly and exclude
it from generic reprocessing. Proposed starting values are:

| Field | Proposed value | Rationale |
|---|---:|---|
| Weapon/animation type | two-handed sword / greatsword | conforms to the list's katana policy |
| Equip type | both hands | removes 1H/dual-wield use |
| Damage | 17 | steel-greatsword-class base, paid for by higher speed |
| Speed | 0.85 | distinctly quick for 2H without retaining the vendor's extreme 1.10 |
| Reach | 1.20 | appropriate for the blade length |
| Stagger | 0.90 | lower impact offsets speed and reach |
| Weight | 10 | lighter than a broad greatsword, not weightless |
| Value | 1,200 | valuable signature loot without an economy-breaking 8,000 value |
| Critical damage | 8 | proportional to the normalized base damage |

Retain `WeapTypeGreatsword`, `VendorItemWeapon`, `MaterialSteel`, and the list's
owned two-handed-katana keyword. Remove `IgnoresNormalWeaponResistance`.

For a player-lootable weapon, reduce absorb health to approximately 5-8 per hit
and give it a finite charge pool around 2,500-3,000. The final magnitude should
be tested with the actual combat and perk stack. An NPC who needs reliable
signature behavior can receive that behavior through the later NPC design; an
unlimited 20-point player enchantment is not an acceptable shortcut.

## Provenance, permissions, and public-pack boundary

The Oldrim page credits models to YYK, Moral Cat, and Beat the Chimpanzee;
textures to YYK; and Creation Kit/translation work to Patrick. Vexcation says
port permission was received by direct message and asks anyone making texture
upscales, bug fixes, or translations to require the original and credit WisNox.
That supports a dependency-based patch, but it is not evidence of a general
open-source license or permission to repackage all original assets.

RisingDragonRonin reports receiving asset permission from
`cnyylzy123456789` and publishes broader Nexus permissions: modification and
credited redistribution are allowed, sale is forbidden, and source credit is
required. That is a more useful basis for a corrected overlay, but it is still
not an OSI license and it does not erase the Oldrim page's separate contributor
credits. Any released derivative must credit all identified contributors and
retain the non-commercial boundary. The relationship between the account named
by Rising and every separately credited source contributor is not established
by the pages reviewed here.

There is also a separate third-party-IP concern. The exact name **Yoto
Hatamonba** and the cursed execution-sword concept match *Hell Teacher: Jigoku
Sensei Nube*: the [current official anime product page](https://nube-anime.com/blu-ray/)
identifies episode 5 as “The Curse of Hatamonba,” and [Apple TV's episode
description](https://tv.apple.com/mx/episode/la-maldicion-de-hatamonba/umc.cmc.7c67mgfuvpxaria5w0n28d7vs?showId=umc.cmc.5geu75jymto62jwhzyv3ksem9)
describes Hatamonba as a cursed sword. The term is associated in secondary
Japanese commentary with an execution place/pole, but no reliable source was
found establishing “Yoto Hatamonba” as the name of a historical sword or an
independent traditional myth.

The prudent conclusion is that the mod's title and premise are probably a
modern fictional reference, not historical provenance. This audit does **not**
claim that the mesh copies the series' visual design; the available files do
not prove that. Mod-author permission also cannot clear rights in an unrelated
franchise. For a public list, keep the art as an external Nexus dependency and
give the owned NPC weapon an original Tamriel/Akaviri name and lore rather than
marketing it under *Nube* terminology.

## Release architecture if approved later

The clean public architecture is a dependency chain, not a modified vendor
installation:

```text
Rising V1 asset archive (immutable; vendor ESP disabled)
    -> Ensrick corrected-NIF overlay (minimum changed asset, credited)
    -> Ensrick signature-weapon ESL (one balanced 2H WEAP; no recipe)
    -> Ensrick named-NPC distribution patch (separate decision)
```

The owned plugin can reference an external mesh path without making the vendor
ESP a master. The asset package remains a documented runtime requirement. This
keeps vendor content out of the owned ESL and makes NPC assignment independently
replaceable. Bundling the corrected NIF should occur only after the user accepts
Rising's attribution/non-commercial terms and the final provenance record is
carried into release documentation.

## Audit limitations

This was a binary/record/asset audit, not an in-game visual test. Plugin records
were examined with source-built Mutagen tooling and full Spriggit serialization;
NIF conversion and validation used the source-built headless `nif-port-cli`.
No official xEdit GUI or game process was launched. Lighting response, hand
alignment, sheath clipping, draw-animation alignment, and actual IED placement
still require a controlled visual test after the weapon, skeleton, animation,
and display stacks are finalized.
