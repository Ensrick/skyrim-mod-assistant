Ensrick Regional Currency Integration 0.3.0

This is a mixed-terms compatibility package for a private Skyrim Special
Edition modlist. It is not an all-MIT work and must not be represented as one.
Every required upstream mod remains a separate dependency unless its author
has expressly permitted the included derived material.

NATIVE LEDGER OWNER

EnsrickCurrencyDenominations.dll is original Ensrick code compiled and
statically linked with CommonLibSSE-NG. The combined DLL is distributed under
GPL-3.0-or-later with CommonLibSSE-NG's Modding and Corresponding Source
exceptions. Exact Ensrick plug-in source and the deterministic build receipt
are bundled under Source/EnsrickCurrencyDenominations. CommonLibSSE-NG
corresponding source is available from the exact public repository and commit
identified in SOURCE.txt. The pinned CommonLib COPYING and EXCEPTIONS texts
and the QuickLoot IE MIT notice accompany this archive.

PAPYRUS COMPATIBILITY FILES

The package contains exactly four compiled Papyrus classes:

- Ensrick_CurrencyRuntimeDefaultsAlias is original policy-enforcement code.
- DES_MadranSwapper is an inert, same-name loader class. The winning quest
  removes the transaction attachment; the class exists only to keep stale
  class resolution harmless.
- DES_DramCurrencySwapper and DES_UlfricCurrencySwapper are narrow M.I.N.T.
  compatibility derivatives. They preserve reviewed cost/stage maintenance
  while intentionally removing every Currency Swapper ownership call.

The two M.I.N.T. derivatives retain their upstream terms and are not
relicensed by the MIT grant. No ECE transaction-handler derivative is included
in version 0.3.0.

M.I.N.T. permission terms were checked on 2026-09-06 at
https://www.nexusmods.com/skyrimspecialedition/mods/178940 . The published
permissions allow credited modification/bug-fix/improvement work and upload
elsewhere, and prohibit paid use. DES_DramCurrencySwapper and
DES_UlfricCurrencySwapper credit M.I.N.T. author Tate Taylor, contain no voice
assets, and remain subject to those upstream terms.

GENERATED ESP AND CONFIGURATION

The generated ESP contains original records and compatibility overrides copied
from Skyrim and required vendor plugins. Vendor-origin fields remain governed
by their respective owners. The patch disables legacy physical-to-ledger
owners, obsolete modern exchange dialogue, non-parity bank recipes, and unsafe
distribution paths; it does not sublicense their source material.

Same-path BOS, CDF, KID, Inventory Injector, translation, and SkyPatcher files
are interoperability overrides. Where they preserve or modify vendor-authored
content, they remain subject to the relevant upstream permissions. Original
late-loading denomination and UI rules are covered by Ensrick's MIT grant only
to the extent they are original.

TIER ASSETS

The local/private package contains eighteen NIF variants and eighteen diffuse
DDS variants derived from separately installed, licensed source mods. These
derived binaries are private modlist outputs and are not granted for public
redistribution by this notice. The public repository may contain only the
deterministic recipe, input hashes, and integration receipt. Original normal,
mask, and environment-map assets are not copied by the recipe; NIF bindings to
the separately installed vendor assets are preserved. Cathedral Assets
Optimizer is not a runtime dependency and does not recreate the tier textures.
