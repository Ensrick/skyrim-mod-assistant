# Dated Skyrim Project Idea Bank

Status: archival brainstorming only

Source date recorded: 2026-08-28

Approval state: The Vast Expanse and Lost Akaviri Island are explicitly
rejected; none of the remaining ideas is an approved requirement,
implementation task, mod choice, or install request

This note preserves older ideas supplied by the user so they are not lost. The ideas may later be revised, expanded, split into separate projects, or dropped entirely. Nothing listed here should be downloaded, installed, distributed, or implemented without a new explicit instruction.

## The Vast Expanse

**Rejected by the user on 2026-08-29. Do not revisit or propose.**

The user previously explored the concept with other mod authors and concluded
that its worldspace, compatibility, and implementation costs were prohibitive.
It is retained here only as a historical decision record so it cannot return to
the backlog as an apparently unresolved idea.

## Armor and Weapon Derivative Variants

Create material, regional, historical, and lore-based variants of strong existing armor and weapon designs.

- Example: reinterpret Nordic Steel Armor in ebony as Nordic Ebony Armor, with attributes comparable to ebony equipment.
- Give noteworthy variants a setting-specific history. One example was a Windhelm smith working with Dunmer craftspeople and Solstheim ebony for the family of a former jarl.
- Where appropriate, alter the visual design as well as the material, then distribute variants by region, faction, encounter level, and relevant added worldspaces.
- Consider high-quality modded designs, including work in the general tradition of NordwarUA, JohnSkyrim, and Billyro, but only with permission and after asset/licence review.
- Explore distinct materials, engravings, markings, and leveled-list integration.
- Do not invest in variants of source designs the user already considers visually undesirable.

## No Mere Bandits

Replace anonymous bandit saturation with identifiable regional organizations, varied relationships, unique equipment, and small stories. The broad concept could integrate added lands such as Wyrmstooth, Falskaar, Beyond Skyrim, and Beyond Reach. Some groups might occupy selected forts or appear through regional encounters rather than merely replacing every bandit.

Related idea: turn the Silver Hand into a credible, organized order of werewolf hunters rather than another bandit presentation.

### Current direction — 2026-08-29

Retain this as a major late-stage project and a central part of the user's
ongoing effort to eliminate generic Skyrim content. Do not begin full
implementation until the mod list and its equipment baseline are substantially
settled.

- Maintain a machine-readable catalog of every adopted armor and weapon source,
  especially standalone equipment. Record asset provenance, permissions,
  redistribution limits, body support, visual identity, material and power
  tier, crafting and tempering behavior, and suitable races, regions, factions,
  levels, and encounter roles.
- Use the catalog to design authored enemy organizations and NPC archetypes
  rather than giving unrelated equipment to generic actors at random.
- Support roaming hostile parties, replacement or alternate occupants for
  selected bandit and Reachfolk forts, and appropriate hostile groups in
  Falmer spaces, Dwemer ruins, Nordic ruins, wilderness encounters, and added
  worldspaces.
- Expand equipment variety for undead, particularly draugr and skeletons, while
  preserving recognizable historical and regional identities.
- Replace shallow vanilla leveled-list repetition with more varied, controlled
  actor and equipment pools.
- Prefer a deterministic, reproducible generator that injects the approved NPC,
  equipment, and leveled-list records into an owned patch. AI may assist with
  catalog classification, proposal generation, and consistency review, but the
  released game data must be fixed, inspectable, repeatable, and testable rather
  than generated nondeterministically at runtime.
- Treat equipment distribution, actor spawning, roaming encounters, and
  location occupation as separate systems. Leveled-list injection can cover
  much of the first two, but taking over forts or placing factions in authored
  ruins also requires deliberate encounter-zone, marker, ownership, persistence,
  quest, and compatibility work.

### Daedric and other cult concepts

- Mehrunes Dagon / Mythic Dawn: hostile cultists, conjurers, Dremora, knights, and Imperial defectors.
- Clavicus Vile: merchants, illusionists, alteration specialists, and tricksters; potentially friendly.
- Eboncrown coalition: a hostile association drawing on Molag Bal, Boethiah, Namira, Mephala, and Sithis, with necromancers, undead, assassins, vampires, cannibals, spiders, and coerced servants. The original note proposed different reactions to vampire characters.
- Azuran priesthood: primarily Dunmer and Khajiit warriors and priests.
- Ironfist Order: Jyggalag-aligned knights and prophets devoted to an ordered void; opposed to Sheogorath and connected to Oblivion-era history.
- Hircine's Hunters Lodge: Nordic and Bosmeri hunters, Reachfolk, werebeasts, witches, hagravens, and dangerous wildlife; possible return of the Spear of the Hunter.
- Malacath's Myrmidons / Ashen Warriors: battle-seeking Orcish-led warriors with some Nordic, Redguard, Dunmer, and goblin members, especially near western Skyrim and Solstheim.
- Meridia's Zealots: undead hunters, clerics, and color-themed Auroran knightly orders.
- The Blighted: Peryite- and Namira-associated plague priests, spiders, and suitable creatures.
- Arachnid assassins: Dunmer- and Argonian-heavy poison users with Mephala, Boethiah, Namira, Sithis, and Dark Brotherhood connections.
- Nocturnal: rare Nightingale encounters.
- Hermaeus Mora: deliberately left open pending a closer review of Dragonborn.
- Sanguine: revelers, smugglers, intoxicants, excess, and criminal activity. Any mature or exploitative material from the old note requires a fresh scope and safety decision before use.
- Sheogorath: Mania and Dementia groups, including Saints, Seducers, and unstable followers.
- Vaermina: dark prophets and dream-related adherents.
- Dawnguard, Vigilants of Stendarr, and an Akatosh- or Blades-associated order were also tentative possibilities.

### Aedric, heroic, and cosmological groups

- Knights of the Nine: wandering knights and occasional squires or retinues inspired by the Relics of the Crusader, with conditional reactions to crime, lycanthropy, vampirism, and Daedric presentation.
- Akatosh Dragon Knights: a broadly friendly concept, not yet defined.
- Auriel's Silver Paladins: rare high-level encounters involving an explained remnant or return of uncorrupted Snow Elves.
- Arkay's Protectors of the Dead: Hammerfell-connected tomb guardians with Egyptian visual inspiration and a possible artifact.
- Dibella's warrior-priestesses.
- Kynareth's druids: Spriggans, animals, shapeshifters, Bosmeri archers, and Nordic rangers; friendly unless provoked or witnessing attacks on peaceful wildlife.
- Julianos-aligned mages and spell-knights.
- Cult of Magnus and the Nine Coruscations: neutral fire specialists, prophets, astronomers, and astrologers.
- Lunar / Shezarr / Lorkhan cult: a Khajiit-led cross-cultural moon priesthood with Dunmer, Altmer, Bosmer, and Imperial members.
- Brotherhood of Talos: Stormcloak-aligned Nordic huscarls, warriors, and elders with knowledge of the Thu'um.
- Greyhelms / the Bull: Morihaus followers connected to the Greybeards, minotaurs, and the Thu'um; anti-Altmer and intentionally exclusionary in the old concept. Possible Lord's Mail connection.
- Order of the Black Dragon / Twilight Riders / Dragon Cult: Alduin worshippers, dragon priests, possible dragon riders, an Atmoran origin, and a belief in draconic rebirth.
- Merchant's Guild: traders, Zenithar clergy, precious metals, and mainly Imperial and Breton membership.
- Reman-inspired Dragonslayers / Disciples of the Dragonborn: Imperial- and Blades-aligned opponents of the Dragon Cult.

### Civil-war and mercenary concepts

- Organized Altmer, Imperial, and Stormcloak platoons.
- Vvardenfell-associated Bosmeri archers with Welsh-inspired northern-ranger styling.
- Dunmer, Khajiit, Alik'r Redguard, Breton, and Argonian mercenary companies.
- East Empire Company forces or contracted personnel.

### Raiders, criminals, creatures, and deserters

- Western Orcish raiders and pillagers.
- Northern Nordic seaborne raiders.
- Black Marsh Argonian assassins and rogues.
- Dark Brotherhood-related groups.
- Khajiit itinerant rogues; the dated source used a term that should be replaced with culturally appropriate wording if revisited.
- Redguard desert nomad raiders.
- Goblins, Rieklings, minotaurs, ogres, and giants.
- Imperial and Stormcloak deserters.

### Reachfolk replacement concept

Replace the vanilla Forsworn presentation—including relevant Witchmen in Beyond Reach—with a more deliberately Celtic-inspired tribal presentation. This is an old creative direction, distinct from any currently installed mesh replacer, and would need cultural, lore, asset-permission, and compatibility review.

## Lost Akaviri Island

**Rejected by the user on 2026-08-29. Do not revisit or propose.**

The former concept for a Japanese-inspired Akaviri island, new lineage, undead,
and associated quest line is retained here only as a historical decision
record. It is not deferred scope and must not return to the backlog.

## Revisit Rules

If any concept is revived:

1. Treat it as a new proposal, not as previously approved scope.
2. Decide whether it belongs in the mod list, a compatibility patch, or a separate authored mod.
3. Recheck current lore, mod availability, compatibility, permissions, and redistribution rights.
4. Define a small testable first slice before attempting worldspace- or faction-scale work.
5. Require an explicit instruction before downloading, installing, editing, or distributing anything.
