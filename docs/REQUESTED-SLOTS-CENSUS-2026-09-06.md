## Method

Convenience sample selected from the existing ecosystem survey, not a ranked or representative sample of all modlists. One export per list. The API's updated date is the export date, not necessarily the release date or proof of current support. Author-verified releases and third-party mirrors are not interchangeable. Nolvus/Wunduniik exports in particular must not be read as official release manifests. Related list families are not statistically independent.

Only exports dated 2025 or later with an available MO2 modlist.txt enter the denominator. Presence and valid text are checked, not export completeness. Count only enabled (+) exact main-folder matches under the aliases below, with numbered prefixes/Nexus-ID suffixes normalized. No patch-only mentions, disabled (-) options, unmanaged (*) rows, or plugins-only inference. A dash means no recognized enabled main folder in this export, NOT proof no bundled/renamed assets exist or that the mod is unsuitable. Main folders do not prove which files win, FOMOD choices, exact installed version, or runtime compatibility.

BCS includes original and 'Book Covers Skyrim Updated' folder families, not PBR/patch/Lost Library-only entries; exact route requires package inspection. Legacy of the Dragonborn bundles BCS, so standalone-folder counts understate book-cover coverage; LOTD is not automatically counted as a separately installed BCS package. HPP and MIC can coexist. CRD is recipe tooling, not a full replacement for Ars/CCOR. Horse follower frameworks are not counted as player horse overhauls.
